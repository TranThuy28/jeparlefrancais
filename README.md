🏫 Bâtisseurs d’Espoir
Slogan: “Bâtir une école, c’est bâtir un avenir.”
“To build a school is to build a future.”

🎮 Game Overview
Bâtisseurs d’Espoir (Builders of Hope) is a 3D third-person story-driven simulation game where players take on the role of a young leader in a non-profit organization. Their mission: to build schools in underprivileged, remote areas and give every child the right to an education.

👥 Target Audience
Youth aged 12 to 25, especially students and French language learners with a passion for education, social impact, and volunteering.

🌍 Story & Setting
The game begins with a handwritten letter from a child in a mountainous village, addressed to the headquarters of Éclats d’Espoir, a non-profit organization striving to bring education to every corner of the world.

Players step into the shoes of a volunteer team leader, embarking on a heartfelt journey through three major regions:

🌫 Misty Mountains

❄️ Frozen Lands

🏝 Distant Islands

Each region represents a chapter in the game, with its own set of challenges, environment, and emotional narrative. Players will connect with locals, raise funds, solve contextual problems, and physically help construct "Hope Schools" tailored for the needs of each area.

🧩 Gameplay Mechanics
🎯 Main Objective:
Raise funds, gather supplies, and successfully build schools in challenging environments.

🕹 Game Type:
3D third-person

A hybrid between narrative storytelling and task-based simulation

🧱 Core Gameplay Loop:
Fundraising at HQ (Éclats d’Espoir):

Talk to donors, organize events, and convince philanthropists to support the cause.

Manage passive fundraising methods (e.g., selling handmade items, French online courses).

On-site Missions (Minigames):

Each location presents a different school-building challenge:

Installing heaters

Tailoring school uniforms

Protecting school furniture from pests

Minigames reward the player with:

Resources for building

Reputation Points, which unlock access to higher-level regions and attract bigger sponsors.

Exploration and NPC Interaction:

Engage in meaningful conversations with local characters.

Collect key story items and context-based tools needed to solve each region’s unique challenges.

Reputation System:

Reputation Points serve as a credibility score.

More reputation = access to harder missions + bigger donors in future levels.

Chapter Progression:

Finish all school-building tasks in one region → unlock the next, more difficult region.

Return to HQ between chapters to prepare for the next mission cycle.

🛠 Development Tools
Game Engine: Unity

Programming Language: C#

🎓 Educational Impact
Through completing the game, players will:

Develop a deeper empathy for children facing educational inequality.

Learn French vocabulary related to education, social work, and child rights.

Improve critical thinking and social decision-making skills.

Recognize the power of community-driven change.

Be inspired to create or participate in real-life initiatives supporting access to education.

🎬 Game Intro & Setting
The game starts with a powerful introductory video, illustrating the reality of global education disparities and introducing the mission of Éclats d’Espoir.

The player meets with the organization’s representatives and sets off on their mission.

🎭 Two Main Settings:
Organization Headquarters:

Talk to passionate educators, plan campaigns, recruit volunteers, and prepare financially and logistically.

Remote Volunteer Sites:

Execute missions based on real-life needs: construction, donations, logistics, and engagement with the community.

🔁 Gameplay Loop Summary
Begin at HQ → Fundraise + recruit

Travel to field site → Complete region-specific minigames

Earn rewards & reputation

Return to HQ → Use new reputation to secure bigger donors and unlock next mission
